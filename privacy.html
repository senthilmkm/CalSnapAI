Effective Date: August 12, 2026

1. Overview

CalSnap AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the CalSnap AI mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Food Photo & Voice Data: When you snap a meal photo or dictate a meal voice note, the image or audio transcript is securely transmitted to our AI serverless proxy for nutrition estimation.
  • User Profile & Biometrics: Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) is processed entirely on your local device via Apple LocalAuthentication APIs and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Local Storage: Your meal log history, customized staples, and personal calorie targets are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.

3. AI Processing & Google Cloud Security

All photo and voice analysis is processed via an enterprise-grade Google Cloud proxy with Secret Manager isolation. We do not sell your personal images or meal logs to third parties. Data processed by AI models (such as Google Gemini) is strictly limited to generating caloric and macro estimations for your personal app experience.

4. Data Retention & Control

You maintain full control over your data. You can configure your meal log retention period (30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or keep forever) or purge all local photo cache and history anytime inside the Settings screen.

5. Educational Purpose Disclaimer

CalSnap AI uses advanced computer vision algorithms to estimate food portion sizes, oil intake, and nutritional macros. These estimations are mathematical approximations intended for general health tracking. CalSnap AI is not a medical device and must not be relied upon for medical dietary prescriptions.
