Privacy Policy

CalSnap AI — Effective Date: August 12, 2026

CalSnap AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Process

  • Food Photos & Voice Notes: Images and brief audio notes you capture are processed in real-time via secure serverless edge proxies to calculate calories and macros. Photos are not saved or shared with third parties.
  • User Identifiers: Anonymous user IDs or Sign in with Apple tokens used strictly to manage your account and sync meal history across your devices.
  • Subscription History: Subscription entitlements and purchase receipts managed securely via RevenueCat and Apple StoreKit 2.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used strictly for App Functionality—enabling food image recognition, updating your daily macro rings, managing Weekly Calorie Banking, and verifying your subscription status.

3. Data Protection & Security

Your API requests are encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.3). The Gemini API key is isolated inside Google Cloud Secret Manager and never exposed on your device.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

We do not display third-party advertisements, track you across other websites or apps, or sell your personal health data to data brokers.

5. Your Rights & Data Erasure

You can clear your local photo cache or permanently delete all your meal logs directly inside the app under Settings → History Retention & Storage.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@calsnap.app
