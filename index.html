Frequently Asked Questions

Is CalSnap AI medical advice?

No. CalSnap AI is strictly designed for educational and self-tracking purposes. It does not provide medical counseling or clinical diagnosis. Always consult a physician for medical dietary guidance.

How accurate is the AI computer vision?

CalSnap AI utilizes Google Gemini 2.0 Flash with 3D volumetric mass estimation. While highly accurate for common meals, interactive sliders allow you to fine-tune exact portion weights in seconds.

Is my photo data secure?

Yes. All API requests are processed through an encrypted Google Cloud proxy with Secret Manager isolation. Your photos are never sold or shared.
