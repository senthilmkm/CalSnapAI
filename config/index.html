Frequently Asked Questions

Is CalSnap AI intended for medical advice or clinical counseling?

No. CalSnap AI is built strictly for educational, informational, and self-tracking purposes. It does NOT provide medical advice, clinical nutrition counseling, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or licensed dietitian regarding dietary decisions or medical conditions.

How does CalSnap AI estimate calories and macros from a photo?

CalSnap AI utilizes advanced sub-second computer vision (powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash) combined with 3D volumetric mass estimation. It recognizes meal components, estimates food density, and calculates calories, protein, carbs, and fat in under 1 second.

What if a restaurant meal has hidden cooking oils or heavy butter?

Simply tap "⚙️ Adjust Portion" on any logged meal card to reveal interactive portion and oil sliders. You can slide the oil slider to recalibrate hidden cooking oils (+10g, +20g oil) with instant live macro recalculation!

How does Weekly Calorie Banking work?

When you eat under your target calories on lighter days (e.g. Monday–Thursday), CalSnap AI automatically saves those extra calories into your Weekly Calorie Bank. You can flex those banked calories on weekends or social dinners guilt-free!

How are my daily calorie & macro targets calculated?

CalSnap AI features an auto-calculator wizard based on the clinical Mifflin-St Jeor BMR & TDEE equation. It estimates your baseline metabolic rate using your age, height, weight, activity level, and weight goal (Lose Weight, Maintain, or Gain Muscle).

Can I export my nutrition data for my nutritionist or coach?

Yes! On the Insights tab, you can export high-resolution PDF health summaries and raw CSV spreadsheets in 1 tap to share directly with your dietitian, doctor, or personal trainer.

How does biometric Face ID / Touch ID lock protection work?

CalSnap AI includes a native biometric lock toggle in Settings. When enabled, opening the app requires Face ID or Touch ID authentication on your device, ensuring your personal food journal remains 100% private.

Is my photo and personal data secure?

Yes. All API communications are processed through an encrypted serverless Google Cloud proxy with Secret Manager isolation. Your photos are never sold or shared with third parties.

What is included in the 7-day free trial?

The 7-day free trial gives you full unlimited access to AI photo & voice meal snapping, Weekly Calorie Banking, portion sliders, glucose stability forecasting, and PDF exports. You can cancel anytime in 1 tap before the trial period ends.
